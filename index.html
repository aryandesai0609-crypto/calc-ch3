Group test tips

  • Split it up: everyone does every problem, then compare. If you disagree, the sign chart decides.
  • Always ask whether the graph is of f, f′, or f″ before you answer anything.
  • Answer the question they asked: the max value (y) vs where (x), and x vs y in optimization.
  • Even powers don't change sign. Check endpoints on closed intervals.
